Product Care & Exchange Policy

Please read this policy before ordering. Keep your invoice; it is needed for an exchange request.

About the jewellery

Our products are fashion jewellery described as 1-gram gold plated, with high-quality, machine-applied micro plating. They are plated jewellery, not solid gold jewellery. Please refer to each product listing and invoice for the exact item description.

Care instructions

  • Keep the jewellery away from perfume, alcohol, sprays, and harsh chemicals.
  • Remove it before bathing, swimming, or applying cosmetics.
  • Keep it dry; after use, gently wipe it and store it separately.

Six-month quality exchange

If the plating fades unusually early or the product has a quality issue, contact us within six months from delivery with your order details and invoice. We will inspect the item and arrange an exchange when the issue is covered by this policy.

  • The item must not be broken, snapped, or physically damaged.
  • Damage caused by perfume, alcohol, chemicals, misuse, or alteration is not covered by this care-based exchange policy.
  • One exchange is allowed per bill/invoice.

Contact us before sending an item back so we can share the return instructions and any applicable shipping cost in advance.

Exchange for another design

Within six months of delivery, you may request to exchange an eligible item for another jewellery item. The exchange value will be 60% of the original item price shown on your invoice, applied as credit toward the replacement item. You pay any remaining difference. This credit is not paid as cash. One exchange is allowed per bill/invoice.

The item must be returned in usable, unbroken condition with its invoice. Contact us first to confirm availability and return instructions.

Your consumer rights: This policy does not limit rights available under applicable consumer law. If an item is defective, incorrect, or materially different from its description, contact us so we can resolve it in line with applicable law.
